# Arduino Mega 2560 3D Case

A two-part 3D-printable enclosure for the Arduino Mega 2560. The case consists of a bottom shell and a top lid, both provided as ready-to-print STL files.

## Files

| File | Description | Size (X × Y × Z) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Arduino-Mega-Enclosure-Bottom.stl` | Bottom part of the enclosure | 107.6 × 59.3 × 16.0 mm |
| `Arduino-Mega-Enclosure-Top.stl` | Top lid of the enclosure | 107.6 × 59.3 × 10.0 mm |

## Printing

- Print both parts separately and in their original orientation.
- Any FDM printer with a build area of at least 110 × 65 mm will do.
- PLA or PETG work well. Choose a heat-resistant material (e.g. PETG or ASA) if the board will run warm.
- Use your slicer's default settings for your printer and material as a starting point, and adjust the tolerances to your printer if the parts fit too tightly or too loosely.

## Assembly

1. Place the Arduino Mega 2560 into the bottom part.
2. Put the top lid on the bottom part.
